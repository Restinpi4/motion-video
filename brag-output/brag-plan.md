## Step 1 rubric

1. **What is the app?** A licensed in-home senior care agency platform (中介) for the US
   where every caregiver visit is recorded on a body camera, AI clips the key moments for
   the family, and the family can watch live.
2. **Most impressive claim?** Hours of recorded care, and the family does not have to watch
   them: AI identifies the key events and uploads the clips.
3. **Visual hook?** A day timeline of a 6-hour visit that the family never sees, then the
   phone where those hours collapse into three clipped moments.
4. **What real UI to show?** The family app: visit in progress → AI highlights → live view
   with an instruction → caregiver booking with reviews → before/after checkups.
5. **Shortest satisfying video?** ~23s: one hook, one reveal, four quick app beats, outro.
6. **Tone?** Preset `polished`. Creative direction: *a quiet, warm product film for adult
   children who worry about Mom: trust, made visible.* Elder abuse is the real pain point,
   so no jokes and nothing graphic; the fear is implied, the product is the relief.
7. **Audio?** Warm, steady music bed with sparse, soft UI accents tied to real on-screen
   actions; one bell on the logo.
8. **Share caption (draft):** You can't be at every care visit. Kinsight records every one,
   and AI clips the moments that matter so you don't have to watch six hours of footage.
9. **User flow worth showing:** visit happens on a body cam → AI clips key moments to the
   family's phone → family watches live and sends an instruction → family picks caregiver
   and time → checkup on day one, follow-up shows progress.

## What is this app?
A licensed in-home senior care platform where every caregiver visit is on camera, AI turns
hours of footage into the moments that matter, and families can watch live.

## The angle
The adult child's quiet fear: *what happens after I leave?* The video never shows the bad
version (the idea's "fast-food smoothie" anecdote stays off screen). It answers the fear
with the product: every visit visible, without asking anyone to watch six hours of video.
Human warmth (serif) vs. the machine record (mono timestamps) is the visual tension.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"You can't be at every visit." Big warm serif on linen, over a mono day-ruler
(9 AM … 3 PM) whose terracotta playhead keeps moving: care is happening, unseen.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A 6-hour visit timeline gets scanned and three moments pop out as clips:
  morning medication, lunch made fresh, shower assistance.
- LIVE view: the family types "Please remind her to drink water." Caregiver replies
  "On it", and a glass of water slides into the live frame.
- Caregiver picker with star reviews; tap Maria, Tue 9:00 AM, "Booked".
- Checkup 1 (free, day one) baseline bars; Checkup 2 bars grow: "Progress report ready".

## Outro / punchline
Wordmark "Kinsight" lands on the strongest music hit. "Care you can see."
Small line: "Licensed in-home senior care".

## User flow worth showing
Recorded visit → AI highlights on the family's phone → live view + instruction →
book your own caregiver and time → before/after checkups. The phone is a fixed anchor on
the right for the whole middle; only its screen and the left caption change.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a quiet, warm product film for worried adult children; trust, made visible
- Interpretation: few words, long holds, soft slides/crossfades staggered (old out, then new in),
  no hard cuts, restrained sound, generous linen space.
