## What is this app?
A licensed in-home senior care platform (中介) for the US: every visit is recorded on a
body camera, AI clips the key moments, families watch live and can instruct the caregiver,
seniors pick their own caregiver and time, caregivers get more orders, and a free checkup
on day one plus a follow-up shows the progress.

## The angle
Same spine as the 23s cut (*you can't be there → now you can see every visit*), extended
into a mini pitch: the fear, the product in use, what each side gets, and why now.
The fear stays implied: the founder's "blended fast-food" anecdote becomes the question
"饭菜用心吗？", answered later by the "午餐 · 现做" clip. Nothing graphic on screen.

## Hook
"你不可能每次都在场。" over the 09:00–15:00 day ruler with a moving playhead.

## Key moments
- Problem: the unseen 5 hours turn hatched; "洗澡认真吗？饭菜用心吗？"
- AI clips three moments; tap "午餐 · 现做" → player with AI tags and key frames
- Live view + instruction → a glass of water enters the frame
- Book your own caregiver and time; free checkup → follow-up progress
- Caregiver side: new orders arrive, the week fills up
- One platform, three winners: 子女 / 老人 / 护理员
- Why now: 婴儿潮一代正在变老 — a street of homes lights up on the beat

## Outro
安见 · KINSIGHT on the 38.20s strong cue; "看得见的照护。" / "持牌上门养老服务平台".
